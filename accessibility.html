Our commitment

We aim for this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. We are still improving, and we treat accessibility as ongoing work rather than a finished checklist.

What we do

  • Use clear, semantic page structure with headings and landmarks, so screen readers can navigate easily.
  • Make navigation, the menu, the bag and forms usable with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines.
  • Label form fields and show error messages in text, not by colour alone.
  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Build layouts that adapt to different screen sizes and text zoom.
  • Keep body text at readable sizes and contrast on light backgrounds.

Known limitations

  • Some small, decorative or uppercase labels may be harder to read for some people. We are reviewing text sizes and contrast.
  • The product carousel on the home page needs its arrow buttons or horizontal scrolling to move between items.
  • Some third-party services we use, such as the Paystack payment window, are outside our control.
