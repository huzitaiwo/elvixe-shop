Who we are

Elvixe (“we”, “us”) sells skincare through this website. We are the controller of the personal information described here. We handle it in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us: your name, email address and message, through the contact form.
  • Notes from Elvixe: your email address, if you subscribe to our occasional updates.
  • Orders: your name, email, delivery details and what you ordered, so we can fulfil and support your purchase.
  • Your account: your sign-in details, if you create an account.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends automatically, such as your IP address and device type.

We never see or store your card details. Payments are handled by our payment provider (see below).

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and support your orders.
  • To process payments, deliver your order and keep records we are required to keep.
  • To send our occasional updates, only if you subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the site secure and working properly.

We rely on your consent (messages, updates), the need to perform a contract with you (orders), and our legal obligations and legitimate interests (security, record-keeping).

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your information. We share it only with services that help us run the store:

  • Google Firebase stores our database (messages, orders, accounts) and handles sign-in.
  • Paystack processes payments. It collects your payment details directly, under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this site, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.
  • Delivery partners receive the name and address needed to deliver your order.

Some of these providers process data outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we rely on their security commitments and the safeguards the law allows.

Browser storage

We keep the contents of your bag in your browser’s local storage so it is still there when you return. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Clearing your browser data removes your saved bag.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up. Order records are kept for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. After that, we delete or anonymise the information.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another service. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

We use reputable infrastructure, restrict who can read stored information, and send data over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we take care to protect what you share.

Children

Our site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please tell us so we can delete it.

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, we will make that clear on the site.
